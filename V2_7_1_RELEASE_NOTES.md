# ROTK v2.7.1 — Five playtest fixes

1. Battle report now shows actual morale and morale damage multiplier at battle start and at each battle round.
2. Attack can be performed with zero officers; optional up to 3 officers consume 1 AP each.
3. City domestic actions do not require or consume hero AP; existing city action lock remains. Search also does not require AP.
4. Persuasion/Hire no longer requires or consumes hero AP.
5. Espionage now displays a result dialog with success/failure, operation, hero, target, chance, cost and outcome.

Hero AP remains required for moving officers, deploying officers in attack, and spying. Movement cooldown unchanged.
