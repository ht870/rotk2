# v2.7.9 — Repelled Attack & Survivor Reporting

- At round 8, an attacker without 3× superiority fails to capture the city; when the defender also lacks 3× superiority and neither army routed, outcome is **repelled / stalemate**.
- Surviving attacking troops return to their origin city and are explicitly shown in battle report.
- Repelled attacks do not capture troops or officers, transfer expedition Gold/Food to defender, or change city ownership. Food consumed during rounds remains consumed; actual casualties remain losses/recovery per existing rules.
- Decisive losses and routs retain the existing capture/loot mechanics.
- Changes applied to both browser local battle engine and server battle engine.
